Official Privacy Policy & GDPR Disclosure Document

GDPR & COPPA Compliant • iOS App Store

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the Avaritia Mod Hub & Companion application ("Application", "we", "us", or "our", Bundle ID: com.avaritia.mod.mcpe.addons) manages user privacy and data security.

We are dedicated to respecting your privacy, protecting personal information, and adhering strictly to international privacy frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Our application operates as an offline-first companion tool and local package installer. We enforce strict data minimization:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, phone numbers, home addresses, or personal credentials.
  • No User Accounts: The application does not require any account registration or login.
  • No Sensitive Data: We never access GPS location, microphone, camera, contacts, or biometric sensors.
  • No Payment Data: The application does not collect payment card information.

3. Local Storage on Device

All user configurations are stored locally inside the application's secure sandbox on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Saved favorite crafting recipes and mod bookmarks.
  • Temporary downloaded addon packages (.mcaddon, .mcpack, .mcworld) staged for direct import into Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
  • User privacy preferences and GDPR consent status.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Telemetry (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing maintenance and provide free access to community addons, the application integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob):

  • App Open Ads & Rewarded Ads: Displayed periodically upon app launch (with a 20-second cooldown period) and when initiating package downloads.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS 14.5+, the application requests permission prior to tracking. If granted, Apple's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) may be used by Google AdMob to deliver relevant advertisements. If denied, non-personalized contextual ads are served without tracking.
  • Diagnostics & Interaction: Anonymous performance telemetry, ad impressions, and crash diagnostics may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR (Articles 15–22)

Users in the EU/EEA and UK are entitled to comprehensive data rights:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): Review all data stored locally by the app.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): Permanently wipe all local caches, bookmarks, and preferences using the in-app "Erase All App Data" function.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): Export your complete local app settings and consent records in structured JSON format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7): Modify or revoke ad personalization consent at any time via in-app Settings.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Our application is designed for Minecraft enthusiasts of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). All mod installations occur through standard iOS document sharing directly into Minecraft on the local device without external child profiling.
